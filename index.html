Augers

Miles of augers

We keep several thousand feet of augers in the yard, from 6″ to 72″, with 3″ to 5″ hexes. If your machine takes a different hex size, we supply hex adapters.

Cutting heads

Tailored to your soil

We have dirt heads, rock heads, and combination heads for jobs where you expect dirt but may hit rock, so we can match whatever your site turns up.

Accessories

Customize your rental

We rent extra track, casing adapters, hex adapters, pipe casing and saddle inserts. If your job needs a part we don't stock, we can fabricate it.

Contracting

Need a crew?

Along with renting equipment, we take on contract work. Our experienced crew installs pipe casing for gas, electrical, sewer and other utility lines, and we bore pipe of any size in any soil condition.

About

Built from scratch, run by family

Vernon Stapleton founded Horizontal Boring, Plus in 1983 without a single piece of equipment. After years of hard contracting work, the business built up one of the larger auger boring inventories around.

Today we have several boring machines in every size, more than three miles of augers, and a yard full of saddles, tracks, casing adapters and hex adapters. That inventory comes with decades of experience, so you can count on us for any auger boring job.

The Stapleton family still owns and runs the business, and we value long working relationships and straight answers.

  1. 1983Founded by Vernon Stapleton, starting with no equipment.
  2. GrowthYears of contracting work build up the machine and auger fleet.
  3. TodayMachines from 20″ to 72″, 3+ miles of augers, and rentals nationwide.
